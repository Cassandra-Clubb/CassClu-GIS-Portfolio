About:

My name is Cassandra Clubb. I earned my Bachelor of Arts in Environmental Science as an ACTC student through Saint Catherine University in May 2021. I am currently pursuing a Master of Geographic Information Science (MGIS) at the University of Minnesota, with an anticipated graduation date of December 2028.

My interests in GIS focus on environmental analysis, conservation planning, and resource management. I am particularly interested in using spatial analysis and modeling to examine land-use and land-cover change, habitat fragmentation, and the impacts of environmental change on ecosystems. I am also interested in applying GIS to identify priority areas for conservation, develop green corridor networks, and better understand wildlife movement and ecological connectivity.

My career goal is to use GIS and geospatial technologies to support decision-making that balances environmental protection with human land needs. I hope to contribute to conservation efforts that promote healthy, resilient, and connected ecosystems.
